FocusFlow Terms and Conditions

Last updated: May 2026

Service Description

FocusFlow is a personal task management and reminder system. It provides daily habit tracking, task prioritization, and SMS-based reminders. This service is for private, personal use by the account owner.

SMS Terms

By configuring SMS reminders in FocusFlow, you consent to receive automated text messages at the phone number you provide. Messages include task reminders, habit check-ins, and calendar event notifications. Message frequency varies based on your settings. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any FocusFlow message or by disabling SMS reminders within the application settings.

Help

For help, reply HELP to any FocusFlow message or contact the application owner directly.

Liability

FocusFlow is provided as-is for personal use. The application owner assumes no liability for missed reminders, lost data, or service interruptions.
