FocusFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

FocusFlow is a personal productivity and task management tool. This application is for private, personal use only. It is not a commercial product and does not collect data from the general public.

Data Collection

FocusFlow stores task and habit data locally on the user's device via browser local storage. No personal data is transmitted to external servers except as required to deliver SMS reminders via Twilio's messaging service.

SMS Messaging

FocusFlow sends SMS reminders only to phone numbers explicitly configured by the account owner. Messages include task reminders, daily habit check-ins, and calendar event notifications. Message frequency varies based on user configuration. Message and data rates may apply.

Data Sharing

FocusFlow does not sell, share, or distribute any personal data to third parties. Phone numbers used for SMS delivery are processed through Twilio's messaging platform in accordance with Twilio's own privacy policy.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the application owner directly.
