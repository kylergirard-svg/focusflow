FocusFlow

A personal task and reminder service operated by Kyle Rocklyn Girard (sole proprietor).

What this service does

FocusFlow is a private task-management and reminder tool built for personal and household use. It sends text message reminders about daily habits, household tasks, and family calendar events, and it lets the account owner text in a task to capture it.

Who receives messages

Messages are sent only to the phone number of the account owner, and to a household member who has asked to be included. FocusFlow does not send marketing or promotional messages and does not message the general public.

How people opt in

The account owner enters their own phone number into the service's configuration. A household member joins only by texting the FocusFlow number first or by asking the owner in person to be added. Nobody is added without taking one of these actions.

Example messages

Morning reminder: "Good morning. Today: Soccer game 9:00 AM. Open FocusFlow to review your tasks. Reply with a task to add it anytime."

Reply to a captured task: "Got it: Schedule vet appointment. Open FocusFlow to sort it into your task list."

Evening check-in: "Good evening. Anything left to capture? Text it now and FocusFlow will sort it in the morning."

Message frequency and opting out

Expect about two scheduled messages per day, plus a reply to each message you send. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.
